---
name: Web3 design handoffs
description: Visual constraints to preserve when delegating work on the Web3 Intelligence interface.
---

When delegating visual work, carry through the project's explicit dark-first palette and readability constraints; do not reduce them to generic direction such as "premium" or "fintech." This app uses midnight, charcoal, and navy surfaces with electric blue/cyan accents and subtle violet, without excessive neon, clutter, or oversized text.

**Why:** A first design pass interpreted a generic visual brief as light paper and green, which conflicted with the product's explicit direction and required correction.

**How to apply:** Include the specific palette and tone in future design handoffs, and compare the resulting styles against `replit.md`.
